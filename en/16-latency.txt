Interrupt mode (MSI)
MSI means message-signaled interrupts; modern devices usually have them enabled by the driver. NVIDIA GPUs often use line-based IRQs; enthusiasts switch them to MSI by hand, but there's no proven FPS gain and a mistake can stop Windows from booting. So WinPulse only shows the mode and changes nothing.
